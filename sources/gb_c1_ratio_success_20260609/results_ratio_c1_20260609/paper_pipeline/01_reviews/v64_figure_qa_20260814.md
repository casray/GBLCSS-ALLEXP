# v64 quantitative figure QA

## Scope

This revision adds two data figures from existing formal experiment records. It does not add new runs or alter any reported table, equation, algorithm step, or reference.

## Figure 4: target-quality diagnosis

- Core conclusion: the formal leave-one-out GB-LCSS targets have lower post-hoc multiclass Brier error than the noisy one-hot targets across the 25 dataset-noise settings.
- Evidence: 125 successful runs, covering five datasets, five noise settings, and five seeds.
- Protocol checks: `leave_one_out=true`, `tie_mode=distribution_only`, and zero modified validation/test labels in every source row.
- Statistics: panel a reports five-seed means with sample-SD error bars; panel b reports the five-seed mean Brier reduction.
- Boundary: clean labels are used only for post-hoc target-quality measurement, not for partitioning, target construction, or training.
- Source data: `figures/source_data/fig4_target_quality_source.csv`.

## Figure 5: grouping implementation profile

- Core conclusion: granular balls provide a fixed-budget shared-region representation whose accuracy and implementation costs vary by dataset relative to exact kNN and k-means.
- Evidence: seed-0 controlled construction/storage measurements and five-seed uniform-0.4 accuracy means on five datasets.
- Results retained in the caption/text: GB is lower in construction time and structure storage than exact kNN on 3/5 datasets; the best alternative is within 1 percentage point on 4/5 datasets; exact kNN is 3.11 percentage points higher on CoraFull.
- Boundary: the timing comparison covers exact kNN only and does not support claims about approximate nearest-neighbor implementations or universal runtime superiority.
- Source data: `figures/source_data/fig5_grouping_profile_source.csv`.
