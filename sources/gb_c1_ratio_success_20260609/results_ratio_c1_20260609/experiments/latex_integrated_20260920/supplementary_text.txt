Appendix D. Additional target-construction analyses
Table 5 of the main paper gives the uniform 0.4 vote and smoothing controls. Averaged over the five noise settings,
the accuracy gain from excluding the current node’s vote ranges from 0.08 to 1.03 percentage points across datasets;
the smallest setting-wise difference is −0.49 points.
With clean training labels (ρ = 0) and otherwise unchanged settings, the accuracy differences from MLP+CE are
−0.24, +2.00, −0.38, −0.87, and −1.75 percentage points on Cora, CiteSeer, CoraFull, Coauthor-CS, and Amazon-
Photo,respectively. Inaballwhosetrainingnodesallcarrythesamelabel,thetargetequalstheobservedlabel. Target
adjustments therefore occur only in balls containing several observed classes.
Tiesamongthemost-votedclassesoccurin123ofthe125dataset-noise-seedrunsandin911ballinstances,affecting
12,788 of 259,000 training-node targets (4.94%). When several classes tie for the most votes, we keep the full vote
distribution instead of choosing one tied class. This prevents the result from depending on arbitrary class numbering.
