Abstract
Graph-free node classification enables prediction from node attributes when graph
relationsareunavailableorcostlytoaccessatdeployment. However,existingnoise-
robust graph methods generally retain graph-dependent inference, whereas graph-
free methods have largely been studied with clean training labels. To address this
gap, we propose Granular Ball Local-Consensus Soft Supervision (GB-LCSS) for
transductivenodeclassificationwithnoisylabelsandgraph-freeinference. GB-LCSS
learnsnoderepresentationsthroughlabel-freegraphcontrastivelearning,freezesthem,
andrecursivelygroupsnodesintogranularballs. Foreachtrainingnode,theobserved
labelsoftheothertrainingnodesinitsballformaleave-one-outconsensus. Aweight
determinedbytheiragreementblendsthisconsensuswiththenode’sobservedlabel
to produce a soft target for a multilayer perceptron (MLP). This design decouples
regionconstructionfromnoisy-labelaggregationanduseslocalevidencetoreducethe
influenceofisolatedannotationerrors. Acrossfivedatasetsandfivenoisesettings,GB-
LCSSachievesthehighestfive-seedmeanaccuracyin24ofthe25settings,compared
withsevennoisy-labelbaselinesthatusethesamedeployedMLParchitecture. Itssoft
targetsalsohavelowermeanBrierscoresthantheobservedone-hotlabelsinall25
settings.
