# Archive Scope

The owner requested archiving GB-LCSS-related historical code, configurations, results,
logs, research notes, negative experiments and manuscript history in this public repository.

On 2026-10-21 the owner selected: upload code/results/records and complete local-artifact
manifests, but keep model weights, array caches and raw datasets on the local machine.

Original experiment files and the original project's Git remote are not modified.
Potential embedded credentials are quarantined rather than published.

This initial policy commit is not a claim that the complete archive has already been uploaded.
Consult README.md, EXPERIMENT_INDEX.md and manifests/ for the completed archive scope.
